# Agency proposal QA checklist

Use this when the public sample-request route has no qualified buyer signal yet and the freelancer positioning needs a safer replacement angle.

## Buyer promise

A 10-minute human-review checklist for small agencies before a proposal goes out. It does **not** log in, scrape, auto-submit, guarantee wins, or touch client/private/payment data.

## 7-point QA pass

1. **Job mirror:** first two sentences mention 3-5 exact job terms.
2. **Specific proof:** one truthful shipped example, metric, client type, or portfolio sample is present.
3. **Scope fit:** the proposal names the first fixed-scope milestone or audit.
4. **Risk reversal:** the buyer can say yes to a small sample/check before a broad retainer.
5. **Human boundary:** no automation, auto-apply, scraping, guaranteed-win, or ROI promises.
6. **Brevity:** 80-220 words unless the client explicitly asked for a longer plan.
7. **Next step:** one clear CTA asking for a sample, call, repo/docs access, or approval to start.

## Signal rules

- **Qualified:** buyer sends paste-safe job/proposal text, requests one sample score, or explicitly asks for the $49 rubric pilot.
- **No-count:** silence, likes, stars, generic “interesting,” or requests for login/scraping/auto-submit/private data/guaranteed wins.
- **Corey hold:** any invoice, payment link, billing, account/KYC, subscription, or recurring spend.
- **Kill/replace:** after 5 no-count outcomes, stop this angle and replace with coach review-copy or repo-maintainer checklist positioning.
